The effects of each component. According to Table 2,
both cycles boost performance over the baseline, but the T-

5. Conclusion
We proposed CC-CDFSL, a self-supervised regularization
framework that enhances local feature alignment in CLIP-
based CDFSL. By introducing the T-I-T, I-T-I cycle, and
complementary Semantic Anchor module, our method ef-
fectively captures fine-grained semantics, improving ro-
bustness and interpretability. Extensive experiments on var-
ious benchmark datasets demonstrate superior performance.
